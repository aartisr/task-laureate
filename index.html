What's inside

Everything a focused team needs

No bloat, no dark patterns, no misleading save states. Every feature earns its place by reducing mental overhead.

✅

Lists, tasks, and priorities

Organize work into lists. Set priorities (low → urgent), due dates, tags, and rich notes. Filter and sort in milliseconds.

↩️

Undo journal for everything

Every task and list mutation is reversible. Create, rename, reorder, complete, or delete — then undo it. No destructive surprises.

🤝

Private collaboration

Share lists and tasks via one-time invitation links. Owner, editor, and viewer roles are enforced at the database level with RLS.

🔔

Reminders that reach you

Assignment-aware reminders delivered in-app, by email, or by SMS. Configurable offsets, per-channel opt-in, and delivery receipts.

🔍

Full-workspace search

Search across all lists and tasks instantly. Activity history shows every change so nothing is ever silently lost.

🎨

Accessible themes

Multiple hand-crafted themes, visible focus states, responsive layouts, and keyboard-friendly navigation.

🔒

Honest about durability

Local, saving, synced, and error states are always visible. The app never claims a cloud save succeeded when it did not.

📦

Workspace export & import

Export your entire workspace as a versioned JSON file. Import it anywhere. Your data is yours — no lock-in.

⚡

Works before sign-in

Explore and create tasks without creating an account. Sign in when you're ready and your drafts travel with you.

How it works

From idea to done in seconds

No onboarding video. No tour overlay. Just a workspace that works.

1

Open the app — no account required

Start creating tasks immediately. Explore the sample workspace to see every feature in action before committing to an account.

2

Create lists and add tasks

Organize work into named lists. Add tasks with priorities, due dates, rich notes, and tags. Keyboard shortcuts keep your hands off the mouse.

3

Sign in to sync and collaborate

Sign in with email or a social provider. Your local work syncs instantly. Share lists with teammates via one-time invitation links.

4

Track, search, and review

Monitor progress, search across everything, review the full activity history, and undo any mistake — at any time.
