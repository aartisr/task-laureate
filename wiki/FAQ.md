# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Task-Laureate free?

The public application is free to use. See the [live app](https://tasks.ai-aarti.com)
for current availability.

## Do I need an app store?

No. Install it as a PWA from the secure website. See [Install Task-Laureate](Install-Task-Laureate).

## Does it work offline?

Previously visited app-shell resources can be available without a connection.
Sign-in, cloud sync, and new remote data still require a network connection.

## Can it manage a whole company?

It is intentionally focused on clear, private list and task sharing rather
than claiming to replace a full organization-wide project-management suite.

## Does AI change my tasks automatically?

No. The opt-in preview presents editable suggestions; a person chooses what to
accept. See [Privacy & Security](Privacy-and-Security).

## Where can I report a problem?

Use [Help & Support](https://tasks.ai-aarti.com/support) or open a focused issue
in the [repository](https://github.com/aartisr/task-laureate).
